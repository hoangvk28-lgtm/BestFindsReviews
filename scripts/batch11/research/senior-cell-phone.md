# senior cell phone research
- 4G LTE bands differ by carrier (AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon); verify bands before buying; some phones are locked to one carrier family.
- VoLTE is voice over LTE; ties to HD voice; support depends on carrier.
- Check HAC rating against the user's hearing aid type.
- SOS button should be separate from keypad, long-press; can trigger in pocket; some call several contacts in sequence.
- Sources mostly retailer reviews; confirm with carrier. Lively Jitterbug is limited to Lively service.
