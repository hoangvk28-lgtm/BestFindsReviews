# portable wheelchair ramp
- Measure rise at the exact spot, include threshold height; underestimating by an inch steepens the slope.
- 1:12 (1 in rise per 12 in run) is the common rule of thumb; a 6 in rise could need 6 ft or more; longer is gentler.
- If between lengths, go to the longer ramp.
- Capacity must exceed user plus chair or scooter plus carried items; many listings state 800 lb.
- Check the mobility device owner guide for incline limits.
- Textured aluminum surface helps in wet; folding ramps trade portability for seams.
- Portable ramps are not a code-compliant permanent ramp; permanent entrances may need landings, handrails, permits.
- OT or physical therapist can advise.
