# personal sound amplifier / hearing amplifier
- FDA treats hearing aids as medical devices intended to compensate for impaired hearing; PSAPs are consumer electronics for non-impaired listening situations.
- Hearing aids (Rx or OTC) are programmed to an individual loss; PSAPs raise volume broadly, often with no frequency fitting.
- PSAP marketing claims are not reviewed; return policies vary.
- High volume near the ear can harm hearing within minutes; start low.
- Anyone noticing hearing loss should see an audiologist or clinician.
