# threshold ramp
- 1:12 slope (1 in rise per 12 in run) is the ADA ceiling for public ramps; ADA does not govern private homes.
- Retail home guidance often cites 1:6 as a practical minimum for short rises; steeper means harder pushing and tipping risk.
- Wedge ramps are fixed: measure exact rise first. Plate/modular ramps adjust, user must pick a safe angle.
- New-construction thresholds are up to 1/2 in; rises over 1/4 in should be beveled.
- Check weight capacity against user plus device; surface grip matters wet or dry; secure or adhesive-fix to prevent sliding.
- Therapist/OT can advise on slope and device fit.
