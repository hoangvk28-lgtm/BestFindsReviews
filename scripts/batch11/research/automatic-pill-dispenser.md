# automatic pill dispenser
- Locked lids are generally recommended where cognitive impairment is a factor; locked compartments keep doses unavailable until the programmed time.
- Alarm models vary: some offer 6 alarms, others up to 9 or 28 daily alarms; check volume adjustability for hearing loss.
- Caregiver alerts via app/SMS can be missed; a phone call alert channel is more reliable. Test the alert path before relying on it.
- Wi-Fi models need home Wi-Fi; Bluetooth models need a nearby phone.
- Check battery backup and low-battery warnings.
- Loading the tray is the hardest step; arthritis and low dexterity matter for setup.
- Dispensers support routine but do not replace clinical oversight; confirm schedule with a pharmacist or doctor.
