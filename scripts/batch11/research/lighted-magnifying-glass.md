# lighted magnifying glass
- Higher power (20x to 45x) forces the lens very close to the page, often 1 to 2 in; 3x to 5x covers a wider field and suits reading full pages.
- Around 3x to 10x is the forgiving range for general reading and daily tasks.
- Larger lens means wider view; very high power lenses are tiny, often under 25 mm.
- Glass lenses resist scratching better than acrylic but weigh more; acrylic is lighter for long sessions.
- Look at LED count, dimming steps and warm vs cool light for white paper vs glossy surfaces.
- Disposable AAA batteries are simple; rechargeable USB-C saves hassle but needs charging.
- Weight and handle comfort matter for long reading; folding handles help storage.
- Be cautious with extreme magnification claims; field of view shrinks as power rises.
- Low-vision aids help with reading tasks only; an eye care professional can advise on needs.
