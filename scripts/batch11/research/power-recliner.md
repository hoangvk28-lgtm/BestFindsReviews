# power recliner
- Wall clearance to recline varies by model, roughly 11 to 17 in on retailer listings; zero-wall designs need less. A glider also needs room to move forward.
- Capacity on glider/swivel listings ranges roughly 250 to 360 lb; confirm the listed figure.
- Foam density around 1.8 to 2.2 and coil or steel suspension are cushion quality signals on listings.
- Power models need a nearby outlet; recline is infinite-position via buttons; they are heavier than manual (roughly 90-130 lb typical).
- Check doorway width for delivery; some chairs need about 44 in for unboxed.
- Spec sources are mostly retailer pages; verify on the dimension sheet.
- A glider recliner is a comfort chair, not a lift chair; it does not raise the user to standing.
