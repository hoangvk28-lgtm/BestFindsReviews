# folding cane research
- Height fit: stand in usual shoes, arm relaxed, handle top at wrist crease; slight elbow bend (about 15-20 degrees) is commonly cited.
- Locking joint is the most important feature of a folding cane; test for wobble when extended.
- Push-button locks are simple but can loosen over time; flip/cam locks allow finer adjustment but can slip if the cam wears.
- Offset handles center weight over the shaft; wide handles spread palm pressure.
- Quad base stands up on its own, adds weight and bulk; single tip is lighter and folds smaller but needs more balance.
- Check rated weight capacity; some wood-handle models cap near 220 lbs.
- One-inch height increments across roughly 29 to 39 inches cover most users.
- A physical therapist can give personalized cane advice.
