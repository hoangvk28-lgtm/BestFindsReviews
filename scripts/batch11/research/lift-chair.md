# lift chair
- Fit first: seat width, depth and floor-to-seat height should match user height and build before extras like heat or massage.
- Typical seat width 20-22 in for average frames; 24 in and up for larger users.
- Standard chairs often rated 300-350 lb, heavy duty 400-450 lb; use the manufacturer's published capacity for the exact size, include clothing and items in lap.
- Single motor moves back and footrest together; dual motor moves them independently (lay flat, feet up); triple adds powered headrest.
- Chairs need clearance behind the back and in front of the footrest when fully reclined.
- If standing from a low sofa is the main difficulty, a secure lift path and sturdy armrests may matter more than full recline.
- An occupational or physical therapist can advise on fit and transfers; individual needs vary.
