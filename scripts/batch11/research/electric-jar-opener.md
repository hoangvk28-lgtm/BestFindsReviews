# electric jar opener
- Most models list 1.2 to 3.5 inch lid range; oversized or oddly shaped lids may not fit.
- Higher-torque claims are marketing; some buyers report weak torque on very tight jars.
- AA battery units: weak batteries reduce turning force; keep spares.
- Generally not for plastic lids or conical jars.
- Electric units are bulkier; check counter or drawer room.
- Lever or under-cabinet openers suit people with some grip left; a jar key can break the vacuum seal first.
- Place opener flat and centered on the lid.
