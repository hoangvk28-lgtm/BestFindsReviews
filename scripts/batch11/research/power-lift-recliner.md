# power lift recliner
- See lift-chair.md: dual motor = independent back and footrest; single motor moves together; triple adds headrest.
- Seat width 20-22 in typical, 24+ for larger users; check capacity includes clothing and lap items.
- Clearance behind and in front needed when reclined; transfers vary by person; a therapist can advise.
- Heat and massage are comfort extras; they do not replace fit.
