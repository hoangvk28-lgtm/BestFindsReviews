# spill-proof-mug research (WebSearch, 2026-10)
- Two large handles and a wide base are the standard stability features for tremor, Parkinson's, or weak grip; oversized contoured handles help with loss of fine motor control.
- Check whether a lid is spill-resistant or only splash-resistant; flow-regulated and spouted lids limit sipping pace and spills.
- Lids are where cleaning gets difficult: pick one that removes and washes easily, and check the dishwasher limit.
- Materials: polycarbonate or plastic is light and unbreakable; stainless steel with a straw is common; ceramic is sturdy and often microwave-safe but heavier and breakable.
- Insulated models keep drinks hot but may not be microwave or dishwasher safe (one listing notes a limit of 82 C).
- Look for a non-slip base so the mug does not slide when set down.
- Aids-supplier brands cost more than marketplace look-alikes, but lid quality varies; claims on "tight seal" are seller-reported.
