# step-stool research (WebSearch, 2026-10)
- Weight ratings seen: ANSI Type IA 300 lb (extra heavy-duty) and Type 3 at 200 lb. Confirm the rating label; do not rely on the listing alone.
- One guide advises a rating at least 50 lb above the user's weight (over 250 lb users); frames rated 400 lb and above tend to use thicker steel.
- Handrail: fixed, welded or bolted, at roughly hip height (about 32 to 40 inches). Detachable rails must lock rigidly before weight is applied; carry handles are not rails.
- Platform: at least about 12 in deep and 15 in wide per one guide; molded texture or replaceable rubber mats outlast painted coatings.
- Base wider than the platform with rubber-capped feet reduces tipping.
- Weight tradeoff: aluminum about 6 to 7 lb is easier to move; steel about 10 to 11 lb is steadier.
- Choose the fewest steps that reach the shelf; a 2-step with a handle beats a tall stool for kitchen cabinets.
