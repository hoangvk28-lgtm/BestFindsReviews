# tablet-stand research (WebSearch, 2026-10)
- Floor stands with overhead arms behave like levers: one guide puts about 4.5 lb as the minimum base for a full-size iPad at maximum extension; listed bases run about 5 to 6.6 lb.
- Cases add weight; manufacturers say heavier devices or cases may need a heavier base.
- Supported screen ranges are typically about 4.7 to 13 inches; some stands top out near 11 inches. Check your tablet with its case on.
- Multi-joint arms (four-joint with 360 degree rotation) let you switch portrait and landscape, but joints must lock without drooping.
- Eye-level matters: a screen held low on the chest pushes the neck forward; raise the screen toward eye level (source is non-authoritative).
- Bed use: pillow-style holders offer fixed angles (listings cite six); arm stands reach farther but need the base pressed against the bed frame.
- For video calls the camera should sit near eye level; low tablets show chins and ceilings.
- Senior needs rarely documented: one-handed adjustment, joints that hold position, reach from a chair. Test before buying.
- Arm load limits are rarely published; check manufacturer specs.
