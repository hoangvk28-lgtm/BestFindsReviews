# lazy-susan research (WebSearch, 2026-10)
- Measure clearance inside the cabinet, not just the door opening; one 12-inch unit fits a standard upper cabinet, a 9.25-inch model fit a tighter spot.
- Full-circle trays use round bearings; D-shape and kidney trays need a bearing with a stop. Match bearing shape and diameter to the tray.
- Steel bearings are common and durable; aluminum is marketed as smoother and quieter.
- Bearing hardware is often sold separately from the tray.
- Listed capacities range from 5 lb to 220 lb for cabinet units; a home table lazy Susan typically holds 50 to 100 lb, commercial tabletop 200 to 500 lb.
- Rotation forces matter, not just static weight; choose capacity well above the load.
- Capacity numbers are seller claims with no common test standard.
- A low-rim tray keeps heavy jars from sliding off while spinning; weight on the edge is the likely wobble point.
- Smooth turning helps users with weak grip reach items without leaning into the cabinet.
