# video-calling-device research (WebSearch, 2026-10)
- Echo Show is the most recommended: voice calling ("call Mom") means no buttons; family can set it up remotely. Needs Wi-Fi and an Amazon account; sizes cited at 5, 8, and 10 inches (2020 sources).
- Facebook/Meta Portal came in 8, 10, and 15.5 inch sizes and used Messenger and WhatsApp; Portal TV was discontinued in 2022.
- GrandPad: big icons, one-touch calls, built-in Verizon 4G (no Wi-Fi needed), 24/7 help button.
- ViewClix-style frames (10 or 15 inch): family starts calls; the senior cannot place them.
- Sources agree: fewer buttons and fewer steps beat feature richness; some learning curve and support are expected.
- Wi-Fi dependence is the main failure point; confirm signal strength where the device will sit.
- Most sources predate recent models and pricing; confirm current availability.
- Match device to person: voice-first if comfortable speaking commands; one-touch tablet if tapping is hard; caregiver-initiated frame for dementia.
