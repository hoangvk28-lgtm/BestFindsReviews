# under-sink-organizer research (WebSearch, 2026-10)
- Measure the P-trap, drain, and garbage disposal first; the gap from basin bottom to cabinet floor sets tier height. L-shaped units slide behind center plumbing.
- Pipe cutouts or adjustable middle tiers fit different cabinets; check door-frame opening width, which is narrower than the interior.
- Metal: powder-coated or rust-resistant carbon steel handles humidity; cheap thin wire rusts.
- Plastic: waterproof but thin shelves sag or crack under gallon-size cleaners.
- Avoid particle board and natural wicker; they warp or rot with leaks.
- Drainage holes help; a base that lifts items off the cabinet floor protects against small leaks (sources do not address this directly).
- Pull-out drawers: double-rail ball-bearing slides glide smoothly; friction slides wear fast. Hinges and wheels should be non-corrosive.
- For older users, pull-out bins reduce kneeling and reaching to the back.
