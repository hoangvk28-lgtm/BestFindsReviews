# drying-rack research (WebSearch, 2026-10)
- Linear feet of rod space is the capacity spec; one guide says about 40 ft handles roughly two loads and 60+ ft suits larger families.
- Wet laundry is heavy: one guide suggests 10 to 15 lb per load as a target; another says 30+ lb rating for standard use and 50+ lb if drying jeans, towels or bedding.
- Listed capacities vary for the same model across sites (e.g. SONGMICS 55 lb vs 77 lb), so verify on the manufacturer listing.
- Ratings assume even weight distribution; wings can sag under very heavy loads.
- Indoor drying should finish within about 24 hours or risk mildew smell and mold (one source); another allows up to three days with rotation.
- Aim for 40 to 60 percent indoor relative humidity; above 60 percent encourages condensation and mold.
- Place near a window or fan, keep items apart for airflow, and skip draping on radiators.
- An extra spin cycle before hanging cuts drying time; a dehumidifier helps if drying indoors often.
- Lighter fabrics dry faster; thick towels and jeans set the real time limit.
