# laundry-basket research (WebSearch, 2026-10)
- Curved hip-hugger shapes (Rubbermaid Hip-Hugger, 2.1 bushels or about 74 L) are the standout ergonomic design; a wider shape may scrape narrow hallways.
- Three-handle designs such as IRIS Hip Grip (48 L) offer grip choices and leave one hand free for a stair railing.
- Capacity: 48 to 53 L suits a typical load; for stairs, 65 to 90 L with a manageable footprint was suggested over a giant bin.
- A 33 L basket holds roughly a medium load of about 8.8 lb (one guide).
- Bigger baskets get heavy when full; judge the loaded weight, not just the empty size.
- Solid-bottom plastic suits damp laundry; open-bottom steel baskets drip.
- Fabric or collapsible baskets need a rigid base or wire frame to avoid sagging.
- Laundry backpacks with padded straps are an option when stairs or hand strain are the issue.
- No source tested grip strength or gave a senior weight limit; check handle comfort in person.
