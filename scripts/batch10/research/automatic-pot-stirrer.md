# Research: automatic pot stirrer (seed)

- Check pot diameter and depth both; capacity in quarts alone misleads.
- Self-adjusting models cover about 6 to 9 inch diameter and 3 to 9 inch depth; some bundles cover wider pots.
- Rechargeable units cost more upfront but save on disposable batteries; run times range from under 60 minutes to many hours.
- Leg heat ratings vary by listing (120C, 125C, 230C seen); confirm before using on hot sauces.
- Silicone or nylon tips protect non-stick coatings.
- Cheaper stirrers can stall in thick sauces and stews; torque matters.
- Cordless avoids cords near a burner.
- Removable dishwasher-safe parts simplify cleaning.
- Stirrers sit inside the pot and can sink or tip in very deep pots.
