# Research: egg cooker (seed)

- Most electric egg cookers steam eggs on a heating plate; the water amount in the measuring cup sets doneness.
- Auto shut-off and a chime signal when the water has evaporated.
- Mineral deposits build on the heating plate; wipe with a diluted vinegar solution (about 1 part vinegar to 10 parts water) and soak around 30 minutes if stubborn.
- Removable, dishwasher-safe trays clean faster than fixed reservoirs.
- Capacity is the key choice: 6, 7 or 9 eggs; cooking fewer eggs than capacity works but needs the matching water level.
- Pierce the wide end of the egg with the pin on the measuring cup so steam escapes and shells crack less.
- Cooling eggs in cold water right after cooking stops the cooking and helps peeling.
