# Research: hand mixer (seed)

- Hand mixers typically offer 3 to 9 speeds; the lowest speed folds in chunky add-ins, the highest whips egg whites and cream.
- Most hand mixers weigh roughly 2 to 5 pounds, with many between 2 and 3 pounds; arm fatigue builds on long high-speed jobs.
- Beaters come as twisted wire, straight wire or flat; twisted wire resists clogging, straight wire suits dense doughs.
- A swivel cord can be locked left or right so the cord stays out of the bowl path.
- Every hand mixer ships with two beaters; some add dough hooks, a whisk or a blending rod.
- Attachment storage cases keep beaters from getting lost.
- Immersion blenders and milk frothers are one-handed but suit liquids and light batters, not stiff dough.
- Hand and stand hybrids add a bowl and base, which adds counter and cabinet footprint.
- Vertical-rest bodies let the mixer stand upright on the counter between steps.
- Dough hooks on a hand mixer suit light yeast doughs only; heavy bread dough strains the motor.
