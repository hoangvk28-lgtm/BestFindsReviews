# Research: electric kettle auto shut off (seed)

- Auto shut-off stops heating at boil; boil-dry protection cuts power if the kettle is empty.
- Cordless means the kettle lifts off a power base, so no cord is dragged across the counter while pouring.
- Gooseneck spouts give a slow, steady stream for pour-over; temperature presets and a keep-warm hold (often up to 1 hour) are common on those models.
- Stainless steel interiors avoid plastic contact with water; glass shows scale and water level but is heavier and breakable.
- Capacity changes weight: a full 1.7L to 1.8L kettle is heavy to lift with a weak grip, while 0.8L to 1L is easier.
- Descale periodically with diluted white vinegar to keep the element and shut-off sensor working properly.
- A wide mouth and removable lid make cleaning easier.
