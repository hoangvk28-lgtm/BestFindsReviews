# Research: electric can opener (seed)

- Side-cutting (smooth-edge) openers cut below the rim, leaving a lid edge that is not razor sharp and can often be reused as a cover.
- Traditional countertop models cut the top rim and need a lever press; hands-free models clamp on the can and run on their own.
- Battery models (often AA) need no outlet or cord; USB rechargeable models need periodic charging.
- Auto shut-off stops the motor when the cut is complete.
- Placement matters: the opener must seat squarely on the can rim or it can stall.
- Magnetic lid lifters reduce direct contact with the lid.
- Removable cutting parts or levers make cleaning easier; food residue builds on the cutter.
- Tall cans and unusual shapes may not fit every model; check can height limits.
