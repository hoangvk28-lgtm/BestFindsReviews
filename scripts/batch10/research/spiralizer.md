# Research: spiralizer (seed)

- Handheld spiralizers work like a pencil sharpener: you twist the vegetable into the blade by hand.
- They suit long, slim produce such as zucchini, cucumber and carrot best.
- Dense roots like sweet potato or beet are harder to turn and can make the vegetable slip.
- Many handhelds have a different blade on each end or a knob for switching modes.
- Some include a finger guard or cap that also covers the blade in storage.
- Handhelds suit occasional users, small kitchens and one or two vegetables at a time.
- Countertop suction-base models handle harder vegetables and bigger batches more reliably.
- Electric models are the better route for limited wrist mobility or hand strength.
- Very fine blades (about 0.5 mm) suit delicate noodles; 2 to 3 mm blades suit wide rustic noodles.
- Choose by use frequency, produce type and storage space.
