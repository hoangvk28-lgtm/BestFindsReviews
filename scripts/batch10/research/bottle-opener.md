# Research: bottle opener (seed)

- Long handles act as levers, so less grip force is needed to break a stubborn cap.
- Adjustable or multi-hole openers cover roughly 0.5 to 3.5 inch caps; check the range against the lids you actually own.
- Rubber lining or hard-rubber faces grip smooth and ribbed caps without slipping; toothed metal grips bite into the cap edge.
- Twist-off caps and pry-off crown caps need different tools; a twist gripper does not lift a crown cap.
- Hook or pry edges can break the vacuum seal on jars before twisting.
- Match the tool to the lids you open most: water bottles, jars, cans or tabs.
- An occupational therapist can advise on tool choice for a specific hand.
