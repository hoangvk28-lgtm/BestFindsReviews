# Research: garlic press (seed)

- Squeeze presses are lever tools: a longer handle arm means less hand force for the same crush.
- Rocker presses are rocked back and forth over a clove and use palm pressure and body weight instead of finger squeeze.
- Tabletop presses use downward body weight and suit limited hand motion.
- A rounded handle that fits the palm is easier than a scissor-style grip that loads the fingers.
- Removable chambers or hinge-free designs make cleaning easier than poking into small holes.
- Stainless steel and die-cast zinc are common; both are durable, stainless suits dishwasher and rust resistance.
- Larger chambers hold several cloves, so fewer squeezes per meal.
- A built-in cleaner or peeler brush reduces fingertip work.
- Studs or spikes matching chamber holes push garlic through with less pressure.
- Peeling is the other hand-heavy step; pressing unpeeled cloves skips it on some models.
- Spreading garlic on a board and rocking avoids squeezing completely but leaves a coarser mince.
- An occupational therapist can advise on tool choice for a specific hand.
