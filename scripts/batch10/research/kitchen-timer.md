# Research: kitchen timer (seed)

- Digital timers count down and up, show exact minutes and seconds, and need batteries.
- Mechanical wind-up timers need no batteries and ring a bell, but are less exact at short intervals and the dial is harder to read precisely.
- Large digits and high contrast displays help anyone who struggles to read small screens.
- Magnet backs and kickstands let the timer sit at eye level on a fridge or counter.
- Alarm loudness matters in a noisy kitchen; a flashing light adds a second signal.
- Auto memory repeats the last set time with one press, saving button steps.
- Fewer buttons means fewer mistakes; check how many presses a reset takes.
