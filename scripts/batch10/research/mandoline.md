# Research: mandoline (seed)

- A guard or food holder should sit between hand and food; many holders grip poorly, so choose one that actually holds the produce.
- Cut-resistant gloves add a second layer, especially when changing, removing or storing the blade.
- Gloves let you skip the holder on slippery or misshapen items, but they are not a substitute for care.
- Stable feet or a non-slip base stop the slicer moving mid-stroke.
- Handheld models hook over a bowl or rest on a board; stand-up models need counter space.
- Built-in or hidden blades reduce blade handling during thickness changes.
- Thickness dials are safer than swapping blades by hand.
- Storing the blade locked or covered prevents accidental contact in drawers.
- Wash blades with a brush, not a sponge held against the edge.
- Short, steady strokes on firm produce beat forcing a soft tomato.
