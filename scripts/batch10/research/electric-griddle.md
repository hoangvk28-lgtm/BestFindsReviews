# Research: electric griddle (seed)

- Cool-touch bases keep the housing exterior cooler than the cooking surface, but the plate itself still gets hot.
- Removable temperature probes allow immersion cleaning of the body in some models; the probe itself must stay dry.
- Ceramic nonstick coatings need nylon or wooden tools and no metal scrapers.
- Surface area decides how many pancakes or eggs cook at once; a larger plate needs more counter and storage space.
- A drip tray or tilt design drains grease away from food.
- Backstop ledges keep food from sliding off.
- Let the plate cool fully before washing to avoid warping.
