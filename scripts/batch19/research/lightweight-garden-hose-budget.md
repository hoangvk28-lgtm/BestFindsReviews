# Research: lightweight garden hoses (budget tiers; also see batch17 lightweight-garden-hose.md)
- Drinking-water use: look for explicit NSF or lead-safe claim, check inner tube; PVC-based hoses draw debate. Not stated by listing = do not assume.
- Aluminum fittings can corrode and fuse to brass spigots; brass or stainless more durable.
- Polyurethane/hybrid light and kink resistant; longer hoses kink more, pick shortest length needed.
- Burst pressure at least about 400 PSI for residential use suggested by a guide.
- Mold reports inside hoses left full in sun; drain and coil after use.
- Warranty terms can be restrictive; some light hoses fail in 2-3 years.
