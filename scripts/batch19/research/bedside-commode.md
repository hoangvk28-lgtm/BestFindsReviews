# Research: bedside commodes (budget tiers)
- Standard capacity about 300 lb; budget listings 300-500 lb; bariatric higher. Check rating on the exact listing.
- Height matters: 1-2 in off is a balance issue. Adjustable range about 18-23 in in one-inch steps on many; some start at 20 in.
- Bucket: look at depth and handle, lid, splash guard. Sizes seen 5 to 7.5 qt. Shallow buckets are messier.
- Steel is sturdier but heavy to carry (about 24 lb in one steel model); aluminum about 14 lb easier to move, cheaper aluminum frames can sway laterally over time (single-source claim).
- Cheap 3-in-1 steel: thin plastic seat with hard edges, stores flat, tool-free assembly. Padded seats (about 42 mm) and backrests appear on higher tiers.
- Drop arms help side transfers from bed or wheelchair; release button on each arm.
- Uses: bedside commode, raised toilet seat, toilet safety frame (3-in-1). Over-toilet use needs bowl-width fit check.
- Check return policy; some medical items are final sale. OT can confirm setup.
