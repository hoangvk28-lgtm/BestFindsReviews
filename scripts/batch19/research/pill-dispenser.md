# Research: pill dispensers (budget tiers)
- Many auto models are 28-compartment carousels for one week at up to four doses a day; alarm counts vary (6 to 28).
- Alarm persistence matters: some sound until pills removed (up to 30 min).
- Locks: key lids to fingerprint; battery backup (AC plus 4 AA) matters for power cuts; battery-only units may lack rechargeable.
- Turning an unit over to silence alarm can jam pills; test mechanism.
- Press-to-open lids help limited dexterity.
- Manual organizers are cheapest but need memory; auto units lock doses behind timers. Some brands need a monthly subscription.
- Humidity, light, heat degrade pills; keep out of bathroom, fill one week, ask pharmacist before leaving original packaging.
- Compartment size vs largest pill (fish oil softgels).
- Alzheimer's Society suggests speaking to a healthcare professional before buying.
