# Research: wireless TV headphones (budget tiers; also see batch17 wireless-tv-headphones.md)
- Lip sync becomes noticeable near 50 ms; plain SBC/AAC Bluetooth often drifts; look for aptX LL or 2.4GHz RF.
- RF: static from microwaves possible; range up to about 300 ft claims vs about 30 ft Bluetooth.
- Optical preferred over RCA/3.5mm; HDMI-ARC-only TVs need an audio extractor.
- Budget units often skip a charging dock (USB cable instead); battery from about 18 h to 40+ h.
- Comfort for 2-3 hour sessions; verify listing latency claims.
