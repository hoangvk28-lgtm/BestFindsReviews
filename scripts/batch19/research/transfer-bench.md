# Research: tub transfer benches (budget tiers)
- Measure: tub inside width at top, tub wall height, clear floor space beside tub (vanity, toilet, radiator).
- Legs sit inside and outside tub; floors often not level, so individually adjustable legs matter.
- Seat height range often about 17.5-22 in; very tall walls may exceed it.
- Sliding-door tracks stop benches seating on the rim; many benches unusable without modification.
- Bench too wide for tub will not sit level; seat blocking drain pools water.
- Features: non-slip feet, armrest, drain holes, reversible backrest, cutouts. Heavier users want margin above rated weight.
- Padded/swivel/sliding versions cost more; basic aluminum benches skip them.
- OT can confirm size and setup; handheld shower makes seated bathing practical.
