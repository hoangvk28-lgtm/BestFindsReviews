# Research: bathtub safety rails (budget tiers; also see batch18 bathtub-safety-rail.md)
- Clamp-on rails fit tub walls about 3 to 6 or 7.25 in; measure rim.
- Flat walls only; curved, sloped, textured edges loosen the clamp. Often not for fiberglass/acrylic tubs.
- Typical rating about 300 lb tested ceiling, balance aid not wall-mounted grab bar.
- Knob tightening needs hand strength; recheck tightness regularly.
- Do not use as towel bar or for hanging loads beyond maker instructions.
