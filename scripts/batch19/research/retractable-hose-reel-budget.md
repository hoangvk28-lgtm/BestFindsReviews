# Research: retractable hose reels (budget tiers; also see batch17 retractable-hose-reel.md)
- Plastic reels are the budget tier: fine for occasional use, lightweight.
- Reported failures on cheap reels: leaks at swivel/hard plastic washer, internal plastic connections breaking, rewind springs failing, sometimes within a month; check warranty and recent reviews.
- Spring spreads hose or housing large enough to hold coil without clumping; lock to stop rewind.
- Wall mount vs portable; metal or fiberglass for frequent use.
- Auto rewind speed can snap fittings back; pull slowly, guide by hand.
- Drain hose before winter freeze.
