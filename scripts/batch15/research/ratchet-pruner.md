# ratchet pruner research
- Ratchet pruners are anvil style: blade crushes against a flat base; good for dead or thick wood, can mash live green stems (roses).
- Capacity listed is the maximum; real ease depends on number of ratchet stages (two squeezes vs three).
- More stages means less force per squeeze, helpful for arthritis and weak grip.
- Anvil capacity ranges roughly 1/2 to 1 1/2 inch.
- Keep blade sharp; a dull anvil blade crushes. Ratchet should release smoothly.
- Handle length around 8 to 10 inch keeps one handed use for precise work.
- Use bypass pruners for live stems; anvil ratchet for dead wood.
