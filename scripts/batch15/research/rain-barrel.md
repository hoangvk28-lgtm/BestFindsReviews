# rain barrel research
- Rain barrel water is non-potable: garden, lawn, car washing; never drinking or cooking.
- Overflow outlet needed; direct overflow away from foundation or link to a second barrel.
- Fine mesh screen over all openings plus a tight lid blocks mosquitoes; mosquitoes need several days of standing water.
- Winterize in freezing climates: drain fully, leave spigot open, reconnect downspout, store indoors or upside down.
- A full 55 gallon barrel weighs over 400 lbs; level, firm base matters; tipping risk.
- Low spigots need a stand or blocks to fit a watering can; elevation also adds gravity pressure.
- Leaf-heavy yards: debris clogs spigot; clean screen.
- Roof area and rainfall decide refill frequency; a small barrel fills fast in a storm, so overflow handling matters.
- Chain barrels with linking kits for more storage.
