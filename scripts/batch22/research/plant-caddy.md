# plant caddy research
- Check capacity against fully watered pot plus plant weight, not empty pot. Guides suggest 150 to 300 lb works for most big specimens; heavy concrete planters need more.
- Match caddy diameter to the pot base; leave room for repotting. Adjustable bases cover a range (e.g. 12.5 to 16.5 in).
- Locking casters hold pots on slopes and busy floors; at least two locking wheels is the practical minimum.
- Budget models: wheels detaching under heavy use are reported; check caster mounting.
- Soft rubber wheels protect tile, hardwood and decks.
- Outdoor: powder-coated steel, aluminum, UV resin or rot-resistant wood; untreated pine can swell with standing water.
- Drainage or a water-catching tray protects floors.
- More wheels spread the load and ease rolling over thresholds.
- Pot shape: a round caddy under a square pot leaves corners unsupported; check contact area.
