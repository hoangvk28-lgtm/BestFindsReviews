# Research: door knob grip (seed)
- Lever handles generally need less dexterity than round knobs and can be operated with a wrist or elbow.
- Knobs are harder for people who cannot close the hand fully.
- Grip covers are a lower-cost alternative to replacing hardware and stretch over the knob without tools.
- Some covers keep center cutouts for keyhole access.
- Add-on lever extensions can put extra stress on the internal latch mechanism, so check fit and how tightly they are secured.
- Check the listed fit diameter against your knob; the search found no authoritative measuring standard, so measure your knob and compare with the maker's range.
- Silicone covers add friction; ribbed texture adds grip.
- Cover choice is about grip force, handle size and one-handed use, not medical benefit.
