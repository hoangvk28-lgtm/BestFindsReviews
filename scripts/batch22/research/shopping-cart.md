# shopping cart research
- Wheels are the biggest quality differentiator: dual front swivel casters turn in aisles; larger rear wheels handle cracks; rubber or foam is quieter than hard plastic.
- Stair climbing carts use a six-wheel rear cluster in an arc so you drag up steps without lifting the load.
- Alloy or tubular steel gives good strength to weight; about 100 to 120 lb capacity with a cart under 12 lb is typical for good carts.
- Match rating to typical load plus about 20 percent margin.
- Adjustable handle roughly 35 to 42 in avoids stooping.
- Check folded size versus car trunk and unfolded depth versus hallways or elevators; a 21.7 in wide wagon will not fit a 24 in doorway loaded with side gear.
- Removable liners help cleaning; waterproof liners protect groceries in rain.
- Fabric wagons (Oxford 300D to 600D) carry bulky items; wire baskets carry heavy compact loads.
- Brakes keep cart still when loading.
