# Research: key turner (seed)
- Key turners add a larger surface to hold and turn, reducing pinching with the fingers and thumb.
- Two main styles: clip-on caps that attach to the key head (Keywing style) and handle-style holders that clamp one to three keys in a handle.
- Handle styles often use a locking lever or screw to hold keys steady; some fold the keys into the handle for storage.
- Clip-ons are usually aimed at flat Yale-style house keys; check key shape and thickness before buying.
- Plastic clip halves can loosen or crack over time per some buyer feedback; keys can shift inside covers.
- Bright colors help tell keys apart; tactile bumps add grip.
- Handle styles suit limited wrist rotation better than small clip-ons because of leverage.
- Check how many keys it holds and whether the key can be swapped.
- Judge grip force, handle size and one-handed use; no medical benefit claims.
