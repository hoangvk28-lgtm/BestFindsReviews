# Research: garden kneelers and kneeling pads
- Pad vs seat: flat mat suits short tasks; knee pads for shuffling along borders; framed kneeler/seat easier for getting up from ground level.
- Foam thickness: about 25 mm minimum on hard surfaces, 40 mm (about 1.5 in) or more more comfortable on gravel or compacted soil; thin pads bottom out.
- Density: dense closed-cell EVA or NBR resists compression and water; soft memory foam can settle.
- Frame: tubing around 0.86 in or thicker resists twisting; check stated weight capacity.
- Handles on kneeler-seat convertibles ideally sit about 18 to 20 in off the ground in kneeler mode.
- Wheels: no reliable external guidance found; rely on listing specs only (wheel size, locking, tool pouch).
- Closed-cell foam wipes clean and does not soak up water; store flat or hung.
