# Research: digital day clock / calendar clock (ease of use, not medical)
- Common guidance: screen at least 7 inches, characters over about 1.2 inches tall, legible from roughly 10 feet; 12 inches or more for wall use or big rooms.
- Spelled-out day and month (not abbreviations) and AM/PM or morning/afternoon/evening labels reduce misreading.
- Dimming: lowest setting should not disturb sleep; auto-dim by schedule or light sensor is useful; simple 3-step dimmers are coarser than continuous or 5-10 level control.
- IPS panels give wider viewing angles than basic LCD.
- Power: most are adapter powered. Battery backup may keep only time/settings through an outage, not the display lit. Check wording on the listing.
- Alarms and reminders: counts vary (5 to 20); reminder labels are text prompts, not monitored services.
- Voice announce on some models reads time aloud on button press.
- Listing claims are maker figures; verify before buying.
