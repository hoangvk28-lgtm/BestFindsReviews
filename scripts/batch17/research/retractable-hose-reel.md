# Research: retractable hose reels (water and air)
- Mounting: wall mount for semi-permanent spot; portable or cart for multiple zones; ground-mount stakes/bases suit places without a wall but are less rigid.
- Check reel rated for hose diameter and length; overfilling drum causes uneven winding.
- Hose bunching on one side or snagging at outlet guide are common failures; levelwind designs help.
- Plastic housings fine for light garden use; steel better for harsh settings.
- Air reels: 10 to 15 m (about 33 to 50 ft) suits single-bay garage; 20 m for walking around a vehicle. 3/8 in ID helps limit pressure drop; reel drum must match hose ID.
- PSI: look for 150 to 200 PSI minimum rated; many listings show 300 PSI working pressure but system is only as strong as weakest coupler. Single-braid rated lower than double-braid.
- Solid mounting and rigid steel frame prevent wobble; swivel bracket lets you pull from several directions.
- Auto-rewind convenient; manual rewind suits heavy tools. Rubber handles cold and abrasion better than PVC.
- Check compressor CFM versus tool demand.
