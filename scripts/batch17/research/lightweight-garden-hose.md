# Research: lightweight and kink-resistant garden hoses
- Longer hoses add weight and friction loss; 5/8 in ID is the usual home balance of flow and handling.
- Materials: polyurethane and hybrid rubber can be much lighter than rubber; TPE flexible in cold; PVC cheaper but crack-prone; stainless spiral is light but stiffer and can keep coil memory.
- Kink resistance comes from material plus wall construction and woven reinforcement.
- Fittings: plastic is the weak point; solid brass 3/4 in GHT recommended; aluminum lighter but galvanic corrosion risk with dissimilar metals; machined or cast brass more durable than stamped.
- Common ID sizes 1/2 and 5/8 in; match fittings and nozzles.
- Verify burst rating and spec on maker data.
